## Description draft
Know your numbers before accepting a load. Truckload helps drivers and small trucking businesses estimate trip earnings and record the costs that affect their bottom line.

- Estimate earnings using load pay, loaded and empty miles, fuel and driver expenses.
- Save trip estimates and enter final costs after delivery.
- Track fuel and DEF purchases, including supported CSV/TSV imports.
- Review rate confirmations from PDFs or photos; verify scanned fields before saving.
- Review weekly earnings and export records.

**Important:** Current records are stored locally on the device. Cloud driver accounts, multi-device sync, live fuel-provider connections and Apple subscriptions are not available in this release.
